UNSIGNED / BETA INFORMATION

Community guide

An invite-only place for independent producers to share music and receive useful feedback.

Sharing music

Share only recordings you own or have permission to share. Link to a supported music service; audio files are not uploaded to SUBLEVEL. Submissions and reviews enter moderation before appearing publicly.

Feedback and rankings

Discuss arrangement, sound design, mix, rhythm and other aspects of the recording. Do not harass people, share private information, solicit votes, or use multiple accounts to manipulate rankings. Each member can rate a recording once, from 1 to 19. Self-ratings are excluded. Rankings require at least five approved ratings and use a weighted average.

Your information

Your handle and bio are public. Approved recordings and reviews are visible to visitors. Pending recordings are visible to their owner and moderators. Choose a pseudonym and avoid adding personal information to your profile or feedback. Passwords are stored as salted hashes; session cookies keep you signed in. Security rate-limit counters help prevent abuse.

Third-party players load when requested and are subject to the music provider's own data and playback practices. Opening an external link leaves SUBLEVEL.

Your controls

Withdraw your recordings from public discovery using your producer profile, or remove your own review from a recording's page. In Account settings, you can change your password, download your own account data, or delete your community account and associated tracks, feedback and reports. External recordings remain on their original services. Sign in to report a submission for moderation.

Beta limitations

This is a limited pilot, not a production service. There is no automatic password recovery. Its free database has a limited lifetime; long-term retention and backup arrangements are not guaranteed. Keep copies of anything important. This guide describes the current pilot and is not a complete privacy notice or legal agreement.
